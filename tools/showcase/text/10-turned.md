# A page that arrived turned

This page is a quarter turn out and has been left that way on purpose.

It came in as part of a scanned bundle from the boatyard, upright on the
glass but with the page instruction saying otherwise, which is what a great
many scanners do and what a great many documents therefore carry for ever.
Nothing is wrong with the words on it. The only thing wrong is which way up
the page says it should be shown.

Turning it back is one button, and it is worth knowing that the button does
not redraw anything. The page instruction changes and the words underneath
stay exactly the bytes they were. That distinction matters most for a scan:
a page that has been re-rendered to straighten it has lost something, and a
page whose instruction has been corrected has lost nothing at all.

## The boatyard's note, as received

Slip repaired to the waterline. Two of the rollers were seized and have been
replaced; the third was sound and was left. The winch cable has about a
season left in it and should be renewed before the spring, not after. Charge
as agreed, less the roller that was not needed.

The yard would like it noted that the tide gauge float was collected on the
same trip and is in their store awaiting the spring crossing.
