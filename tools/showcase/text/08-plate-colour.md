# Plate IV

## Colour, transparency and stroke

Four washes of the boat house paint, laid over one another as they would fall
on a wall that has been painted twice and touched up three times. The rings
are drawn at fifty-five parts in a hundred, so that what is underneath still
counts. Everything on this page is drawn rather than photographed: a circle
here is an instruction to the reader's machine, not a grid of dots, and it
stays sharp at any size a printer or a screen cares to ask for.
