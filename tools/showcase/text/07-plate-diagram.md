# Plate III

## How the tide gauge works

Sea water enters the stilling well through a narrow inlet at the foot. The
inlet is deliberately too small to pass a wave: the level inside the well
follows the tide and ignores the chop, which is the whole trick. A float on
the water carries a shaft; the shaft turns a counter; the counter writes one
number to a card every minute. Nothing in the chain needs power except the
counter, and the counter runs a year on two cells.
