# Declaration

Four undertakings, a box to tick, and somewhere to sign. The panel takes a
signature drawn or captured on the machine, and the warden will take a typed
name from anybody whose machine cannot manage that.

1. I will keep to the marked paths above the tide line between the fifteenth
   week and the twenty-second, which is when the ground is nesting.
2. I will carry my own rubbish off the island, including the rubbish I find,
   which is a request rather than an undertaking and is usually agreed to.
3. I understand that the crossing is weather dependent, that the boat is not a
   ferry, and that a booked departure is a hope rather than a promise.
4. I have read the fire notice in the bunkhouse, or I will read it within an
   hour of arriving, which is the same thing said honestly.
